# Changelog: The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild 4:3

Newest first. Each release's section becomes its GitHub release notes.

## v1.0.0

For game v1.6.0.

First release.

- The 3D view renders at exactly 4:3.
- HUD, menus, loading screens, title screen, dialog boxes and the Sheikah Slate map are re-laid out for the 4:3 screen: elements sit at the real screen edges, full-screen layers cover the whole screen, and nothing is stretched.
- The gameplay HUD (hearts, Divine Beast gauges, weapon and shield icons, sensor, noise meter, temperature and clock) is shown at the same size as on 16:9, and the stamina wheel keeps its round shape.
- Adventure Log: quest text, the quest list and Memories have the correct shape, and long quest descriptions are no longer cut off.
- Map: terrain, place names and icons stay lined up while you move and zoom, and the marker pointing to Link only appears when he is off screen.
- The speaker's name plate sits on its dialog box.
